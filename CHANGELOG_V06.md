# The Living Counter — V0.6 Expressive Dialogue Hotfix

- New fantasy visitor **Liu Xue**, a dynasty-era noblewoman who understands English but replies mostly in Mandarin with occasional broken English.
- Transparent VN portrait set for Liu Xue: neutral, thinking, amused, skeptical, annoyed.
